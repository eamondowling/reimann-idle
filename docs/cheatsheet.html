Riemann Idle, field notes for failing efficiently

Every number on this page comes straight from the game's code. Time estimates come from simulating a player who always buys the upgrade that raises progress rate the most per failure spent, and never clicks after the first 15.

ζThe loop

AttemptA click or an auto-attempt. Each one either makes a little proof progress (rare) or logs a named failure.
FailFailures are the currency. Upgrades cost failures, taken proportionally from every type in your catalogue.
UpgradeSpeed upgrades add attempts per second. Counter upgrades raise your progress odds. Every upgrade can be bought again and again, and each level costs more than the last.
Paradigm ShiftAt 1% proof progress, prestige. Progress and attempt count reset; failures and upgrades stay.
Two ways to go faster: more attempts, or better odds.

Grad Student, Computer Access, Supercomputer Time and Quantum Computer add attempts per second. Counter upgrades cut a failure type's odds, and 5% of the odds they remove becomes progress chance, shown in the Progress Odds stat. The best play mixes both: by the first Paradigm Shift a good build has lifted progress odds from 2.0% to about 4.5%. The catch is that prevented failures aren't earned, so heavy countering slightly slows your upgrade income.

∂Counter upgrades

Each level multiplies the named failure's odds by (1 − reduction), and levels compound. Every failure type starts at 1/27 odds, and 5% of whatever you remove turns into progress odds. Example: one level of Temperature = 0 removes 80% of 1/27 ≈ 2.96%, adding about 0.15% to progress odds (2.0% → 2.1%). Counters that hit two failure types give the most per level.

∞Offline and saves

The game saves to your browser's local storage ten times a second. When you reopen it, time away is credited as auto-attempts, capped at 8 hours, and a toast tells you how many ran. Clicks don't count while you're away, so buy at least one Grad Student before leaving. Clearing site data, private windows and switching browsers or devices all start a fresh save; there is no export.

∴Fastest route to color

  1. Click 15 times and buy a Grad Student.
  2. Early on, buy speed upgrades with the best APS per failure. Once you have a few speed levels, start mixing in counters. Their value grows with your APS, because better odds multiply every attempt you make.
  3. Leave the tab open or come back within 8 hours so no offline time is wasted.
  4. Paradigm Shift the moment you hit 1%. Each shift is permanent speed and makes the next 1% much cheaper.
  5. Third shift: every illustration in the last-failure panel switches to its colorized version.
